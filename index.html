Inverse Problems Group

We study the mathematics of recovering causes from effects: uniqueness and stability of inverse problems, regularisation of ill-posed equations, and fast numerical methods for inverse scattering and imaging.

Research

What we work on

Four directions, one common thread: ill-posedness and how to tame it.

Inverse scattering

Recovering the shape and material of an unknown object from far-field or near-field measurements. Qualitative methods, sampling methods, transmission eigenvalues.

Coefficient inverse problems

Determining PDE coefficients from boundary data. Carleman estimates, convexification, global convergence without a good initial guess.

Regularisation theory

Convergence rates for Tikhonov and iterative regularisation, parameter choice rules, and statistical inverse problems.

Computational imaging

Fast solvers and reconstruction algorithms for tomography, ultrasound and electrical impedance imaging, including learned regularisers.
